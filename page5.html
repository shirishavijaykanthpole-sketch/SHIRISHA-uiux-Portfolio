Home's App

Re-architecting urban apartment discovery and verified roommate requests for bachelors and students navigating dense housing ecosystems across India.

/ 01. The Challenge Overview

Addressing the Urban Housing Bottleneck

Finding affordable, reliable, and bias-free living spaces remains a critical systemic failure all over India, particularly in thriving academic and technological hubs like Pune. Bachelors, remote workers, and university students routinely encounter extreme real estate profiling, opaque price inflation, and structural safety anxieties.

The current rental ecosystem forces individuals to depend on disjointed online group feeds or arbitrary local brokers who exploit the market via unregulated fees. The core design challenge centered on building an end-to-end framework capable of rendering verified, peer-reviewed properties while completely eliminating unbacked rental friction layers.

/ 03. Roommate Pairing & Safety Metrics

Peer Discovery & Structural Verification

A major feature engineered for Home's App is the interactive roommate discovery node. Users looking to split space allocations in a precise geographic sector can quickly drop an active request detailing localized criteria.

Interested parties interact through an automated, structural safety checklist. This built-in workflow guides both users through thorough background verification metrics, lifestyle parameters, security documentation, and legal guidelines to maintain absolute trust before contract generation takes place.

/ 04. Possibilities of Market Success

Disrupting Legacy Real Estate Networks

The platform's growth potential lies in its disruption of structural financial drag. By maintaining a strict no brokerage policy across primary listings—and limiting minimal brokerage variables exclusively to highly dense, specialized target zones—Home's App instantly captures user trust.

By providing verified peer authentication systems, safe algorithmic filtering, and transparent lease configurations, the design addresses the exact societal bottlenecks that current industry legacy platforms systematically fail to solve.
